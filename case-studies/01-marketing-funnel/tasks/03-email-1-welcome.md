# Task 03: Write Email 1 — Welcome and Value Proposition

> This is a project task file. Read `00-REFERENCE-rules.md` before starting.

## What To Do
Write the first email in the 5-part nurture sequence. This email fires immediately after form submission. Its job is to confirm the subscriber made a good decision, deliver any promised value, and set expectations for what's coming next. The tone should be warm, direct, and credible.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. Write a subject line (max 50 characters per Tier 3) that feels personal, not promotional. Avoid spam trigger words.
2. Open with a one-line acknowledgment: the reader just signed up, and that was smart.
3. Deliver the core value proposition in 2-3 sentences. Mirror the language from the landing page headline (Task 01 output). Lead with their problem (Tier 4: COPY-METHOD), not your solution.
4. Set expectations: "Over the next few days, I'll share [what they'll learn]." Keep it to one sentence.
5. Include ONE soft CTA — either reply to the email or visit a resource. Do NOT push the booking page yet (that's Email 5).
6. Add email footer: unsubscribe link (Tier 1: CAN-SPAM) and physical mailing address (Tier 1).
7. Total word count: 150-300 words (Tier 3: PLATFORM-LIMITS).

## CRITICAL — Tier 1 Compliance Check
- Scan all text for banned phrases from the reference file
- Verify no income claims or implied earnings
- Confirm unsubscribe link is present
- Confirm physical mailing address is present
- Verify all social proof references real, verified client results only

## Do NOT Touch
- Landing page copy (Task 01/02 output)
- Email platform configuration or template settings
- Booking page URL or calendar integration
- Any other email in the sequence (Tasks 04-07)
