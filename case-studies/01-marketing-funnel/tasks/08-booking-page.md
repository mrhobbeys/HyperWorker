# Task 08: Write Booking Page Copy and Confirmation Message

> This is a project task file. Read `00-REFERENCE-rules.md` before starting.

## What To Do
Write the copy for the booking page (where the reader schedules their discovery call) and the confirmation message they see after booking. The booking page must feel like a natural next step from Email 5, not a hard sell. The confirmation message should reduce buyer's remorse and set expectations for the call.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Booking page headline:** Write a headline (max 12 words, Tier 3) that reinforces the value of the call. Frame it around what the reader gets, not what you offer.
2. **Booking page body:** 2-3 short paragraphs (Tier 4: short paragraphs, 1-3 sentences). Cover:
   - What happens on the call (set expectations)
   - How long it takes (respect their time)
   - What to prepare (if anything)
3. **Social proof element:** Include one brief proof point near the calendar embed. Must be from a real client (Tier 1).
4. **Calendar embed placement:** Note where the calendar widget goes in the layout. Do not configure the calendar — just indicate placement.
5. **Confirmation message:** Write a short (50-100 word) message that appears after booking. Include: confirmation of the booking, what to expect before the call (prep email?), and a warm sign-off.
6. **Confirmation email subject + body:** Write a brief confirmation email (100-150 words) that mirrors the on-page confirmation. Include the call details and any prep instructions.

## CRITICAL — Tier 1 + Tier 2 Compliance Check
- Zero income claims or earnings implications on the booking page
- No pricing mentioned (Tier 2: pricing discussed on calls only)
- No fabricated testimonials
- Unsubscribe link in confirmation email
- Physical address in confirmation email footer

## Do NOT Touch

- Email sequence (Tasks 03-07)
- Calendar integration settings
