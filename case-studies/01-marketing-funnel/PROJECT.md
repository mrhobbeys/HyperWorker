> This is a project file managed by HyperWorker.

## Status
IN PROGRESS

## Objective
Launch a 3-step lead-generation funnel (landing page → 5-email nurture sequence → booking page) that converts cold traffic into discovery-call bookings within 2 weeks.

## Scope

### Included
- Landing page (headline, subhead, form, social proof section)
- 5-email welcome/nurture sequence
- Booking page with calendar integration
- Thank-you / confirmation page

### Explicitly Excluded
- Paid ad creative (separate project)
- CRM setup or migration
- Blog content or SEO pages
- Social media posts promoting the funnel

## Key Constraints
- All copy must pass Tier 1 (BRAND-ABSOLUTE) review — no income claims, no fabricated testimonials
- All content saved as DRAFT — human reviews and publishes
- Emails must comply with CAN-SPAM requirements
- Landing page must load in under 3 seconds on mobile

## Dependencies
- Domain and hosting active
- Email platform account configured
- Calendar booking tool connected
- Brand guidelines document available

## Completion Criteria

- [ ] 5 emails written, sequenced, and reviewed
- [ ] Booking page copy complete and reviewed
