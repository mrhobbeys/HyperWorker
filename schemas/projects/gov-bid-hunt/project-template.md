## Phase shape

**Phase A — Setup.** Confirm registration/eligibility status (OR-001) and inventory the portals/sources that post this segment's work (SRC artifacts).

**Phase B — Discovery.** Sweep live sources for currently-open opportunities; capture each as a Finding with fit rationale; qualify and prioritize (include / watch / exclude with reason).

**Phase C — Pursuit.** Prepare a reusable capability statement; make bid/no-bid Decisions on the top opportunities; draft the strongest response; update the tracker; hand off.

## Hard scope boundaries

- Only opportunities with a response deadline AFTER today are "open"; past-deadline items are excluded-after-discovery with the date noted.
- No fabricated solicitation numbers or deadlines — unverified fields are marked "unconfirmed" and verified before any bid effort.
- Out-of-segment opportunities are handed to the correct segment project, not pursued here.
- Do not route work to, or rely on, any entity the operator has marked EXCLUDED in OR-001 (e.g., sold or divested entities).
- Operator approval required before any bid is actually submitted.

## Completion criteria

- Registration/eligibility status recorded in OR-001.
- Every monitored portal registered as a SRC artifact.
- Every discovered opportunity captured as a Finding with status incorporated (pursuing) | watch | excluded with reason.
- Top opportunities have an explicit bid/no-bid Decision.
- Tracker tab updated; session handoff written.
